What is Tilli?
Tilli is a whole-child development platform for schools. We measure the foundational social-emotional and cognitive skills that shape how a child learns, relates, and copes — and turn that into clear, term-by-term insight teachers can act on. The goal is simple: every child developmentally on track by the time they reach middle school.
Who is Tilli for?
Children ages 3–12, and the schools, teachers, and families who support them. Teachers and coordinators use Tilli to see where each student stands; leaders use it to understand whole cohorts; families get a window into how their child is growing beyond grades.
What exactly does Tilli measure?
12 foundational skills across executive function and social-emotional learning — attention, working memory, cognitive flexibility, inhibition (distraction and response), planning & organisation, emotion awareness, emotion regulation, empathy, relationship skills, metacognition, and critical thinking. Each is measured from more than one perspective, term after term, so you see real growth rather than a one-off snapshot.
How is this different from a regular test or survey?
Tilli isn't an academic exam and it isn't a single questionnaire. It looks at the developmental foundations that sit underneath academic performance, gathers evidence from multiple angles, and tracks each child over time — so the result is a direction of travel and a next step, not just a score.
How does Tilli work in a school?
Schools begin with a short teacher training, then run brief measures each term. Results appear on a dashboard alongside practical, classroom-ready next steps for each child and each class — with baseline, midline, and endline points across the year to show progress.
How much time does it take?
It's designed to fit real classrooms. Onboarding is a single short training, and the termly measures are brief — Tilli fits around teaching rather than adding a heavy new workload.
Does Tilli fit our curriculum?
Yes. Tilli complements IB, Cambridge / IGCSE, CBSE, ICSE, and state-board settings. Because it measures developmental foundations rather than subject content, it works alongside whatever curriculum your school already runs.
Is Tilli research-backed?
Tilli is grounded in developmental psychology and the learning sciences, built with researchers and refined through real partnerships with schools. See Tilli for Research for the approach, and Success Stories for what it looks like in practice.
Where does Tilli work, and in which languages?
Tilli partners with schools across South Asia — including India and Sri Lanka — and is built for multilingual, mixed-ability classrooms. If you're wondering whether Tilli fits your context, get in touch and we'll talk specifics.
How is student data handled?
Student data is kept private and used only to support the child's development — never sold. You can read the full details in our Privacy Policy.
